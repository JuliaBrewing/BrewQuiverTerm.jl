Tutorial: a batch from scratch

This tutorial builds an English bitter from an empty quiver, using only the keys of the app: water, malts, hops and yeast (defining them as we go), the stages of the vessels, and the arrows between them; then it edits it, undoes, draws it, looks at its timeline, solves it for a gravity, and saves it.

The screens below are those of the app itself, drawn while this page was built: the keys are given to it as you would type them.

Once defined, an ingredient is found by its name or a part of it: pale2 1 kg pale would take the same malt. Salts and acids are always known (gypsum 4 g gypsum), and a library of ingredients can be loaded with L.

Stages

The stages of each vessel are empty nodes, filled by the arrows: a label and a vessel.

for (label, v) in (("mash", "mashtun"), ("rested", "mashtun"), ("kettle", "kettle"),
                   ("wort", "kettle"), ("chilled", "kettle"), ("fermentor", "fermentor"),
                   ("beer", "fermentor"))
    press!(app, "as"); answer!(app, "$label $v")
end

Arrows

c connects the node of the cursor to another, with a transfer of everything unless a kind of arrow is given (mashrest, drain, boil, chill, ferment, prime, ...). The quiver must brew after each edit, so the yeast goes in before the fermentation:

for (src, to) in ((:strike_water, "mash"), (:pale, "mash"), (:crystal, "mash"),
                  (:mash, "rested mashrest"), (:rested, "kettle drain"), (:cascade, "kettle"),
                  (:kettle, "wort boil"), (:wort, "chilled chill"), (:chilled, "fermentor drain"),
                  (:yeast, "fermentor"),
                  (:fermentor, "beer ferment"))
    select!(app.session, NodeRef(src))     # (the cursor; with the keys: ↓ ↑)
    press!(app, "c"); answer!(app, to)
end

The batch brews, chilled to 20 °C before the fermentor: with no sparge, 16 L of water give a small, strong wort, which the next section fixes. The ⚠ on the mash is a warning of its mash pH: crystal malt was given no distilled water pH (the inspector, Enter, says so).

The nodes that changed are marked ✱, and the status bar tells what u would undo. On an arrow, e offers its kind and its fields, e.g. the time of the boil:

select!(app.session, ArrowRef(:kettle, :wort))
press!(app, "e1"); answer!(app, "70 min")
press!(app, "u")
app.message
"undid Set the time of :kettle => :wort to 70 minute"

Every edit can be undone (u) and redone (U); h shows the history:
