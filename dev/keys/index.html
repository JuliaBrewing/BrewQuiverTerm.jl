Keys

The keys of the app, as ? shows them:

using BrewQuiverTerm
println(BrewQuiverTerm.Term.remove_markup(BrewQuiverTerm.HELP))
Moving
  ↓ ↑  j k        next, previous node (in the overview), scroll (in the inspector: j k move)
  PgDn PgUp       ten nodes down, up
  g G  Home End   first, last node
  Tab  Shift-Tab  the node and the arrows leaving it
  Enter  i  →     inspect the node or arrow
  Esc  q  ←       back to the overview

Editing  (each edit can be undone; the nodes it changed are marked ✱)
  e               edit: a lot (amount, ingredient; water: volume, temperature, profile),
                  or an arrow (its kind, or a field)
  a               add a stage, a lot of an ingredient (munich 0.5 kg Munich malt), or water
                  (strike_water 16 L 75 °C); an unknown ingredient can be defined there
  E               the equipment of the vessel of the node
  L               load a library of ingredients (a BeerJSON document)
  c               connect the node to another: its label, and the kind of arrow (wort boil)
  r               rename the node
  v               the vessel of the node
  x  Delete       remove the node (asks first) or the arrow
  u  Ctrl-Z       undo
  U  Ctrl-Y       redo
  h               the history of the edits

Views
  d               the drawing of the quiver (l arranges the nodes), or back to the overview
  t               the timeline: when each vessel is busy (+ - zoom on the brew day)
  T               solve: targets (node quantity goal), vary, as many parameters:
                  fermentor gravity 1.050 sg, wort ibu 30 IBU vary pale crystal, cascade
                  (lots scaled together, or a field of an arrow: kettle>wort.time)

Brewing
  M               the models: bitterness, color, ABV, gravity (undoable)
  I               the inventory: what the brew needs, with the stock of the library
                  (C takes it from the stock, W writes the library)
  X               export the recipes, as BeerJSON

Files
  s  Ctrl-S       save
  S               save as
  o  Ctrl-O       open a quiver, or import a BeerJSON recipe

  ?               this help
  q  Ctrl-C       quit (asks about unsaved changes)

What is typed

Values are read with parse_value of BrewQuiverUICore, the inverse of how they are shown: 4.8 kg, 17 L, 95%, 70 min, 4 L/h, 1.050 sg, 80 °C, any unit of Unitful or UnitfulBrewing, a plain number for a count or a fraction, a timing (10 min remaining, at 55 min), or nothing (empty, –) to leave a field to the vessel or the default.

promptwhat to typeexample
add a stagelabel, and vesseltrub kettle
add a lotlabel, amount, ingredient (or a part of its name)munich 0.5 kg Munich malt, yeast 1 US-05, gypsum 4 g gypsum
add waterlabel, volume, and temperature (20 °C if not given)strike_water 16 L 75 °C
a new ingredientits properties, separated by commasfermentable: yield 80%, color 9 °L (also potential, moisture, di_ph, buffering, kind); hop: alpha 5%, form pellet; culture: attenuation 78%, kind ale, form dry
connectthe destination, and the kind of arrowwort boil
water profileions and their concentrations, in mg/Lcalcium 50, sulfate 100
solvetargets (node, quantity, goal), vary, as many parametersfermentor gravity 1.050 sg, wort ibu 30 IBU vary pale crystal, cascade

In a solve, the quantities are gravity, og, volume, ibu, srm (or color), abv and carbonation; a parameter is lots scaled together (pale crystal), or a field of an arrow, kettle>wort.time or cascade>kettle#2.amount for the second of two arrows.
